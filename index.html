Why Relay

Lead routing shouldn't live in scripts nobody can read

Most teams route leads through a mix of assignment rules, flows and custom code. It works until someone needs to change it.

Without Relay

  • Routing logic is spread across rules, flows and scripts.
  • Every change is a ticket to the platform team.
  • You find out a rule is wrong after leads go to the wrong rep.
  • Nobody can say which version of the rules is running.

With relay.

  • One workflow you can see, on one canvas.
  • Sales ops make the change, with a checkout so edits never collide.
  • Test on sample and recent leads, then Dry run before anything is assigned.
  • Every publish is a numbered version, and the status shows which one is live.
How it works

  1. Design

  2. Test

  3. Publish

Routing

One switch, three modes, one honest status

Routing evaluates unassigned leads that change after you turn it on. Existing leads are never backfilled.

Off

Nothing is evaluated. Where every workflow starts.

Dry run

Leads are evaluated and Relay logs who it would assign. Leads and round-robin turns are never changed.

Live

Relay writes the owner or group with a conditional update, and records a conflict if the lead changed meanwhile.

Not running means a mode is on but something it needs is missing. The page names it and links admins straight to the fix.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first

Will Relay reassign leads we already have?

No. Routing only evaluates unassigned leads that change after you turn it on. Existing records are never backfilled.

What exactly does Relay change on a lead?

Only the Owner or Lead group field, and only in Live mode. Dry run logs the assignment it would make and changes nothing.

Can two people edit the routing at the same time?

No. One person checks out the workflow and is the only one who can change, publish or discard it. Everyone else sees it read only until it's published or released.

What happens when no rep is eligible?

The path you design decides. A typical flow keeps the account owner when one is eligible and falls back to a round-robin pool or an assignment group when not. Tests show the outcome before you publish.

Does any data leave our instance?

No. Relay runs entirely inside your ServiceNow instance, so your lead data stays where it already is.
